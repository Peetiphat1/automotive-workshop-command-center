Automotive Workshop Command Center

Complete User & Operations Manual for the All-in-One Workshop Management Platform and R.O.S.E. AI Copilot. Covers vehicle intake, automated diagnosis, inventory control, calendar scheduling, P&L reporting, and customer tracking.

1. System Overview & Architecture

Engineered to streamline auto repair operations, eliminate paper bottlenecks, and provide real-time visibility across all workshop activities:

AI Diagnosis & Pricing

Extract vehicle symptoms and calculate accurate labor and replacement parts estimates via R.O.S.E. AI Copilot.

SQLite Database

Single-file database (workshop_demo.db). Fast, resilient, zero-maintenance, and effortless to back up.

LINE & Public Tracker

Real-time vehicle status tracking accessible by vehicle owners 24/7 without requiring phone calls.

2. Getting Started (Launch & Stop)

3 Easy Ways to Launch

1

Launch via Desktop Shortcut (Recommended)

Double-click the "Automotive Workshop Command Center" shortcut on your Desktop.

2

Launch via Direct Batch File

Double-click "START Automotive Workshop.bat" located on your Desktop.

3

Launch from Project Directory

Double-click "START.bat" (or run.bat) inside the root project directory.

💡 Note: Once launched, the server initializes and automatically opens your web browser to the Command Center at http://localhost:8000/dashboard.
🛑 How to Stop: Simply close the black Command Prompt console window, or press Ctrl + C inside the terminal.

4.1 Command Center Dashboard

4 Real-time KPI Cards

Top metrics update continuously based on workshop operations:

  • Total Revenue This Month: Summation of labor fees and parts billed across all approved tickets.
  • Active Repairs: Total count of vehicles currently in repair, plus count awaiting mechanic approval.
  • Low Stock Warning: Items dipping below their minimum reorder thresholds.
  • Today's Appointments: Scheduled arrivals for today. Click directly to open the booking calendar.

R.O.S.E. AI Copilot (Right Dock)

Ask questions in natural language or execute quick workshop actions:

> "How much 5W-30 engine oil is left in stock?"
> "Is repair ticket #12 approved yet?"
> "Show revenue breakdown for this month"

4.2 Service Tickets & Intake System

New Intake Workflow

1

Click "+ New Intake"

Located on the top right header or on the Repairs management tab.

2

Enter Vehicle & Complaint Details

Input license plate number, customer name, contact phone, and customer-described symptoms (e.g. "Front brake squealing, spongy pedal feel").

3

AI Auto-Calculates Labor & Parts

R.O.S.E. AI extracts structured symptoms, maps required replacement parts from stock, and computes initial labor and parts pricing.

4

Approve Work & Finalize Repair

Technician lead clicks "Approve" to initiate work. Once physical service is finished, mark as "Complete Repair" to update inventory and log earnings.

4.5 Stock & Inventory Management

Access via the "Stock" sidebar tab or direct URL: http://localhost:8000/stock

📦 Parts Catalog & Categorization

Manage parts by SKU, category (Fluids, Filters, Brakes, Electrical, Suspension), wholesale cost, retail price, and reorder warning points.

🔄 Fast Adjustments & Audit Trail

Click [+] to receive stock or [-] to withdraw parts, with mandatory audit remarks recorded for every inventory shift.

4.6 Purchases & Parts Suppliers

Manage vendor relationships and purchase invoices:

  • Suppliers Directory: Store vendor contacts, addresses, representative phone numbers, and payment terms (e.g. Net 30 days).
  • Purchase Bills: Log incoming stock orders, track payment obligations, and verify fulfillment.

4.7 Finance & Expense Tracking

Keep workshop cash flow healthy with accurate overhead bookkeeping:

💸 Daily Expense Logging

Record overheads: electricity, water, internet, facility rent, tools & machinery, technician wages, and shop consumables.

📈 Automated P&L Statement

Revenues automatically offset against cost of parts and operating expenses to calculate net monthly profits instantly.

4.8 7 Standard Management Reports

1. Sales Report

Daily and monthly revenue breakdown by labor vs. replacement parts.

2. Top Parts

Rankings of most frequently consumed and profitable spare parts.

3. Stock Valuation

Total asset value of stock on hand (Cost Price vs. Retail Value).

4. Mechanic Performance

Jobs completed per technician, labor billings, and commission shares.

5. Top Clients

VIP customer leaderboard by total cumulative spending.

6. Profit & Loss (P&L)

Revenue minus Cost of Goods Sold minus Operational Expenses = Net Profit.

7. Sales Tax Report (VAT)

Calculates taxable base and output VAT (7%) ready for monthly tax return filing.

4.9 AI OCR Scanner (Bills & Plates)

Access via direct URL: http://localhost:8000/scan

🚘 License Plate OCR

Snap or upload vehicle front photos. The OCR automatically recognizes registration numbers and province to auto-fill repair tickets.

🧾 Purchase Bill OCR

Upload supplier receipts. AI parses line items, unit costs, and automatically reconciles them with inventory stock.

5. Troubleshooting, Backup & Data Migration

Common Issues & Resolutions

!

Port 8000 Already in Use

Open Command Prompt, run netstat -ano | findstr :8000, identify the PID, and execute taskkill /PID <PID> /F, then re-run START.bat.

!

Database Backup & Device Transfer

All data is housed in workshop_demo.db. Use the top database icon 💾 or navigate to Settings ➔ Backup & Data Migration to download a snapshot or import onto a new computer. Automatic safety backups are created before any restore operation.
